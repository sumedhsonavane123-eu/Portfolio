Know the layout

Buda is hills. Pest is pulse.

The Danube splits the city in two, and the two halves feel completely different.

West bank

Buda

Green hills, old streets and castle views. Quieter, more residential, and best at sunrise or sunset.

  • Castle District (I): Matthias Church, Fisherman's Bastion, the palace
  • Gellért Hill (XI): the Citadella and the best panorama
  • Víziváros (I/II): riverside cafés below the castle
  • Óbuda (III): Roman ruins and a village feel
East bank

Pest

Flat, grand and loud. Boulevards, markets, theatres, cafés and nearly all the nightlife are here.

  • District V: Parliament, the Basilica, Váci utca
  • District VII: the Jewish Quarter, ruin bars, street food
  • Andrássy út (VI): UNESCO boulevard of the Opera and mansions
  • City Park (XIV): Heroes' Square, Széchenyi Baths, Vajdahunyad Castle

Survival Hungarian

Say it, and watch them smile

Hungarian is famously hard, so locals love any effort. Tap a phrase to copy it. Most people in tourist areas speak English.

Good to know: Hungarians put the family name first, as in "Kovács János". "Szia" (see-ya) is a casual hello and goodbye. Use "Jó napot" (good day) with strangers.
