About

Mumbai to Europe, by way of EU institutions and youth diplomacy.

I'm Sumedh, a Master's student in Industrial Management & Innovation at Hochschule Mittweida (HSMW), Germany, with a double degree at Deutsch-Kasachische Universität (DKU) in Almaty, Kazakhstan. Originally from Mumbai, India, I moved to Germany in 2023 for this degree and have spent the time since building a practice in EU policy, youth diplomacy, and public affairs alongside it.

My current work sits across three organisations: I'm a research assistant with Democracy International's We The Peoples Campaign, assessing candidate states for a potential UN reform coalition; a communications and research intern with Empowered Human Rights (Let Them Learn Consultancy); and a volunteer policy contributor with the German-Baltic Future Foundation. I'm also an alumnus of the European Student Assembly, where I delegated in 2025, co-authoring two adopted policy recommendations, and returned in 2026 as an Alumni Advisor.

My Master's thesis, submitted May 2026, is a comparative study of how youth-led diplomacy initiatives across four European institutions translate policy recommendations into real institutional impact, the same question that runs through most of the work above.
