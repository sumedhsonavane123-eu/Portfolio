ESA 2025: Delegate

Co-authored two policy recommendations on youth mental health, officially adopted and presented to MEPs.

OrganisationEuropean Student Assembly
RoleDelegate
FocusAdvocacy & Diplomacy
LocationEuropean Parliament, Strasbourg

Context

Turning lived experience into two adopted recommendations.

As a delegate at ESA 2025, I worked with fellow students to draft policy recommendations on youth mental health, one focused on stigma and early intervention, the other on migrant and exchange student mental health support. Both were officially adopted and presented directly to Members of the European Parliament.

Adopted recommendations

Breaking the Mental Health Stigma: Youth-Driven Advocacy and Early Intervention

Youth-led social media and street campaigns to normalise seeking psychological support, mental health screenings folded into school health checks, and parent education programmes.

MovingMinds EU: Ensuring Mental Health Support for Migrants Through Orientation, Therapy Access, and Multilingual Services

Educational sessions for migrants and exchange students, multilingual 24/7 helplines, intercultural peer mentorship through universities and ESN, and subsidised therapy for low-income immigrants.
