Context

A week on media literacy, democracy, and AI.

"Young, Informed and Involved: Strengthening Democratic Literacy and Media Participation" was a study session held at the European Youth Centre in Strasbourg, organised by European Youth Press with the DYPALL Network and the Council of Europe's Youth Department. It brought together 39 young people from 22 countries to examine how media literacy, democratic participation, and the responsible use of artificial intelligence can strengthen young people's role in civic life.

The week combined interactive workshops, group discussions, simulations, and peer-learning activities, grounded in non-formal education principles, pairing theoretical input with hands-on exercises like newsroom simulations and storytelling work.
