Context

Supporting the German Country Office.

I contribute to the German Country Office under the Head of Country Office and the core team, supporting civil society outreach and network engagement across Germany and the Baltic states.

What I do

Erasmus+ KA210 Project Proposal

Leading the coordination of a trilateral Erasmus+ KA210 project proposal between DBJW, VOKI (Verein für osteuropäische Kooperation und Integration), and YATA Belgium, a cross-border youth dialogue initiative on EU enlargement and disinformation resilience, with outputs to be presented to policymakers in Brussels.

Ukraine Open Letter Campaign

Contributing to the Ukraine Open Letter campaign: analysing the letter, identifying key criticisms and counter-arguments, drafting a three-phase launch strategy, and mapping network contacts as potential signatories.
