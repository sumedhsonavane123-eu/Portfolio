ESA 2026: Alumni Advisor

Panel 8: "Europe's Strategic Autonomy: How can the EU reconcile strategic autonomy with its core values in the field of trade agreements?"

OrganisationEuropean Student Assembly
RoleAlumni Advisor & Organising Team
FocusAdvocacy & Diplomacy
LocationEuropean Parliament, Strasbourg

Context

Guiding the next cohort through a hard question.

Returning to ESA as an Alumni Advisor after delegating in 2025, I guided Panel 8 through the EU's structural dependencies (defence, critical raw materials, digital infrastructure, and financial systems) and how strategic autonomy can be reconciled with the EU's core values in trade policy.

Alongside advising, I joined the organising team coordinating logistics for the full parliamentary session.

What I did

From weekly drafting sessions to the Parliament floor.

01

Weekly drafting sessions

Guided 30–35 participants through structured sessions addressing EU dependencies in defence, raw materials, digital infrastructure, and finance.

02

Policy drafting support

Helped shape recommendations spanning sovereign infrastructure, defence operationalisation, and trade oversight.

03

On-site coordination

Supported logistics for 200+ student delegates during the 3-day session, including security checks and Parliament navigation, working with European Parliament staff.

Key recommendations shaped

Sovereign infrastructure

Recommendations on sovereign payment and cloud infrastructure to reduce EU digital dependency.

Defence operationalisation

Proposals for operationalising Article 42(7) TEU on mutual defence assistance.

Critical raw materials

A strategic reserves recommendation to reduce exposure to external supply shocks.

Capital Markets Union

Proposals supporting deeper EU financial market integration.

Trade policy oversight

Recommendations for stronger democratic oversight in EU trade agreements.
